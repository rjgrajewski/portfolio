# Rhymind — business layer

Rhymind is a side project Rafal built end to end on AWS — his own
product, his own architecture, his own deployment pipeline.

Why it is worth discussing in a hiring conversation:

- It shows **full ownership**: not a slice of someone else's system, but
  the whole thing — problem, design, build, ship, run.
- It is where he practised the habits that show up in this portfolio:
  AWS-native, serverless, dev/prod separation, cost kept near zero when
  idle.
- It is a demonstration that he ships personal work to completion, not
  just to a README.

What Rhymind actually does, who it is for, and where it is in its
lifecycle are to be filled in for Phase 8. Ask for the technical layer
for the architecture.
